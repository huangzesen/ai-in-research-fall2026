## Tentative outline

This course introduces the foundations and practical use of large language models (LLMs) and AI agents in research. In the first meeting, we'll explain at a high level how LLMs generate responses, how agents use tools and coordinate tasks, and where these systems help or fail. We'll also ask participants about their backgrounds and interests so we can adjust the emphasis of later sessions.

Over the quarter, we expect to explore:

- **How LLMs and agents work**, from prompting and tool use to multi-agent coordination.
- **How to use them efficiently:** choosing an appropriate model and workflow, balancing quality, speed, and cost, and checking results.
- **Common failure modes**, including unreliable answers, brittle tool use, and evaluation pitfalls, and how to detect and mitigate them.
- **Hands-on research examples** and the role of benchmarks in measuring progress. Interested participants may explore contributing useful evaluations.

The sequence and depth will stay flexible based on participants' interests. No prior AI expertise is assumed.
